Agoda 10.0 Exceptional

“Exceptional stay”
We had a wonderful two-night stay at MyBanLao, and it ranks among the best experiences we've had in Southeast Asia—having traveled to over 95 countries. The staff took exceptional care of us, from our check-in to our departure. We stayed in a courtyard room that featured an open shower and bathroom concept, a free mini bar stocked with soft drinks, strong hot water, effective air conditioning, reliable WiFi, and a good selection of English channels. Typically, we don't make much use of hotel amenities, but everything at this hotel made it easy and enjoyable to do so. We especially enjoyed the spa treatments, including a great full-body massage for $20 (60 minutes), as well as complimentary access to the steam room and jacuzzi. The happy hour by the pool, the free shuttle to town, the EV rental for $40 (10 hours), and the free airport transfer—which was the most comfortable we experienced during our six days in Laos—were also fantastic. We even took a sunset dinner cruise with River Sun Laos for $45. Overall, everything felt seamless and luxurious during our stay.

Agoda 9.6 Exceptional

“Excellent ”
Amazing hotel with amazing staff. There was no aspect of my stay that wasn't exceptional. The facilities are immaculate with plenty to keep you occupied - whether you want to unwind by the beautiful pool, relax at the spa or even explore the turtle rehab centre. The hotel's location makes most things very walkable but, if you're not up to it, there is a complementary shuttle service to get you to the city centre and back. They will also provide transfers to/from the airport or train station. The courtyard suite (which I was upgraded to) was large, extremely comfortable, and clean. Everything felt state of the art. The staff was always very attentive and responsive. They're able to arrange excursions or point you in the direction you need to go. Food at the restaurant was great as well, and service at the spa was excellent.
